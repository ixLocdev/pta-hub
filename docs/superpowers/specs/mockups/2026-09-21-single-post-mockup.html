Class Parents 2026–2027

We still need class parents.

Thank you to every family who signed up in the first week of school. We’re not quite there yet: several classes still don’t have their two class parents.

If you’ve been meaning to put your name in, now is the moment — signing up takes about a minute on your phone.

The draw · Thursday, September 10

Sign up by end of day Wednesday, September 9

Two class parents per class are chosen at random on Thursday. Everyone selected is notified by email.

§ How it works
1

Sign up

Fill in the short form — your name, your email, and your child’s class. That’s it.

2

The random draw

This is not first come, first served. On Thursday we draw two parents at random for every class, so signing up late is no disadvantage.

3

You hear from us

Everyone selected is notified by email with next steps. If you aren’t drawn, we keep you on the list.
