Where do I park for drop-off?

1 min read·Updated September 21, 2026

Drop-off is at the north entrance on Elm Street, between 7:40 and 8:05.

Pull all the way forward to the cone before letting anyone out — it keeps the line moving and it's the single biggest thing that shortens the wait for everyone behind you.

If you arrive after 8:05 the gate is closed. Park on Elm and walk your child to the front office to sign in.

Spirit Week, start to finish

6 min read·Updated September 18, 2026

Spirit Week is five themed dress-up days, one per school day, usually the week before a big fundraiser. Nothing about it costs money.

Six weeks before

Pick the five themes and get them approved by the principal. Avoid anything that needs a costume somebody has to buy — the whole point is that a kid with nothing in the closet can still take part.

  • Book the morning announcements for each day.
  • Ask the art teacher about a hallway banner.
  • Tell the office so the front desk can answer questions.

Two weeks before

Put it in the newsletter with the themes listed by day, and send the same list home on paper. Families plan the weekend before, not the night before.

The week itself

  1. Monday — take photos in the morning, while everyone still looks the part.
  2. Wednesday — check in with teachers; participation always dips midweek.
  3. Friday — the big one. Save the most popular theme for last.

The day after

Write down what worked while you still remember it, and put the photos somewhere the yearbook people can find them.
